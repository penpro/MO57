### New-game load 24.5 s -> 7.1 s (afternoon): far voxel chunks were looking for lakes smaller than one of their voxels
**Diagnosis:** the generator's water pass (`FMOWorldGen::ApplyWater`) resolved every basin tier at every LOD. A far voxel chunk samples ~1 km apart, so each sample landed in a different upland-lake cell and ran `ComputeUplandLake` (hundreds of full-detail base samples) for lakes ~260 m across -- invisible at that spacing. A 32 km LOD-11 chunk took 5 s to mesh; meshing groups chunks by column and runs a column serially, so two such chunks made the voxel world's second state take 10.4 s on ~3 cores, and every PCG chain waits for the voxel state (`WaitForVoxelWorld`). The first full state cost 2.3 min of CPU for the same reason.
**Fix:** `FMOWorldGen::IsWaterTierResolved(Params, Tier, StepCm)` -- a tier is resolved while its largest radius is wider than the sample spacing (the octave rule, wavelength < 2 x step, applied to a basin's diameter). `ApplyWater` takes the step and skips unresolved tiers; the terrain node paints the lake-bed surface only where the tier is resolved. `MO.WorldGen.DebugNoLodCull 1` still resolves everything.
**Evidence (packaged, seed 1029024224):** READY **24.5 -> 7.1 s at 1920x1080**, 25.2 -> 7.1 s at 960x540, 7.3 s on a third run. Full voxel state 8.2 s / 2.3 min CPU -> 0.8 s / 12.6 s CPU; the 10.4 s second state -> 0.3 s. Census within 100 m of spawn: 7916 instances, unchanged (sink / tilt unchanged). `MOFramework.PCG.SampleCosts` COARSE CHUNK: the LOD-11 chunk's 1089 columns in 0.6 ms. Test `MOFramework.WorldGen.WaterLodGate` (rule per tier; a real upland lake carves at the voxel size and is absent past every tier's spacing). Shots from 1.6 / 6 / 12 km and a 1.5 km-high overview: the near lake shows water in its bowl with the bed rim, the far terrain shows no pits (`Saved/StagedBuilds_DevTest/Windows/MO57/Saved/Screenshots/Windows/HighresScreenshot0000{0..3}.png`).
**How it was found:** `voxel.LogStateTime 1` (state wall vs CPU time) + `-DumpVoxelStatus` (pending voxel tasks) + a new local diagnostic in the voxel plugin, `voxel.render.LogSlowChunkMs <ms>` (logs every render chunk / meshing column slower than that: LOD, key, bounds). The readiness trace now also prints `voxelNewState` / `voxelPending`.
**Negative result:** bypassing `WaitForVoxelWorld` on the near tiers (`Content/Python/bypass_voxel_wait.py`, report-only now) changed nothing (READY 24.6 vs 24.5 s) -- the near cells arrived with the voxel state either way. Reverted.

  - Content edits do not need an editor restart.
  - The `ui` instance runs fine next to the editor for VISUAL checks, but never for perf: they share the GPU.
