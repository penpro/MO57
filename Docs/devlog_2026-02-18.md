# MO57 Development Log - February 18, 2026

## Creature AI & Character Systems Update

This update introduces a complete creature AI system and character appearance framework, laying the groundwork for wildlife encounters and character customization.

---

### Creature AI System

Wildlife is coming to the world! We've implemented a full behavior tree-based AI system for creatures:

**Prey Behavior (Deer, etc.)**
- Creatures detect players through both **sight and hearing**
- When threatened, they **flee** to safety at sprint speed
- Prey animals have wide peripheral vision (~150°) making them hard to sneak up on
- They **remember threats** even after losing line of sight, continuing to flee
- Day/night cycles affect behavior - creatures **rest during the day** and **sleep at night**

**The Technical Bits**
- `MOCreature` base class inherits full medical/anatomy systems from the player character
- `MOCreatureController` manages AI perception with configurable sight radius, hearing range, and threat memory
- Activity states (Active, Resting, Sleeping, Fleeing, Fighting, Dead) drive both AI decisions and animations
- Footstep noise system - faster movement = louder footsteps = easier detection

**Behavior Tree Nodes Created:**
- Flee From Threat - finds safe location away from danger
- Wander - peaceful roaming within home territory
- Rest/Sleep - time-based rest with interrupt on threat
- Attack - for predators (coming soon)
