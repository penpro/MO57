## Findings

- Sound (Wes: "sound is broken, video, in game, everything; a scratchy noise as scenes change"): found two causes in the code, neither reproducible with the `-nosound` instances. (1) The master volume was applied TWICE:
  every sound class was set to category x master (`UMOAudioSubsystem`: Music / Ambient / SFX / UI class volumes, the weather volume broadcast, the intro video volume) AND the audio device's transient primary
  volume (which multiplies every sound) was set to the master too. Wes's saved settings are Master 0.49, Music 0.80, SFX 0.157, Ambient 0.181: music played at 0.80 x 0.49 x 0.49 = 0.19 and SFX / ambient at
  0.038 / 0.043 (about -28 dB): inaudible. Now the master is applied once, at the device (`ApplyMasterVolumeToAudioDevice`); test `MOFramework.Audio.MasterVolumeAppliedOnce`. Wes's own Options sliders are still low
  for SFX / ambient (his file: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MO57\Saved\Config\Windows\GameUserSettings.ini`; not touched). (2) `AudioNumBuffersToEnqueue=1` (one 1024-frame buffer = 21 ms of slack): any render-thread
  stall longer than that (a level change, a Nanite / shader burst) underruns the device = scratchy bursts "as scenes change". Now 2 (the engine default). `MO.Audio.Diag` / `MO.Audio.Tone` are there for the next report.
