================================================================================
TECHNOLOGY ERAS
================================================================================

Era 1: Early Paleolithic (2.6M - 300K BCE)
  - Hammerstones, sharp flakes, crude choppers
  - Basic foraging and scavenging

Era 2: Middle Paleolithic (300K - 50K BCE)
  - Flint knapping, hand axes, scrapers
  - Fire making, hide processing
  - Hafted spears, digging sticks

Era 3: Late Paleolithic (50K - 10K BCE)
  - Bow and arrow, atlatl
  - Bone tools, sewn leather clothing
  - Fishing equipment, microliths

Era 4: Mesolithic (10K - 6K BCE)
  - Polished stone axes, dugout canoes
  - Composite bows, advanced traps
  - Basket weaving, bark containers

Era 5: Early Neolithic (6K - 4K BCE)
  - Pottery (hand-built)
  - Grinding stones, sickles
  - Early farming tools

Era 6: Chalcolithic (4K - 3K BCE)
  - Spinning and weaving
  - Native copper working
  - Cloth clothing

Era 7: Early Bronze Age (3K - 2K BCE)
  - Copper smelting, bronze alloying
  - Bronze tools and weapons
  - Leather armor, wooden shields

Era 8: Late Bronze Age (2K - 1.2K BCE)
  - Wheel-thrown pottery, kilns
  - Bronze armor (helmets, greaves, scale)
  - Chariots, glass making

Era 9: Iron Age (1.2K BCE - 500 CE)
  - Iron smelting (bloomery)
  - Steel making (carburization)
  - Chain mail, advanced smithing
